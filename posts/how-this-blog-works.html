How this blog works

Sep 28, 2026

There's no static site generator, no build step, and no database. Every post is just an HTML file in the posts/ folder.

Adding a post

  1. Copy this file, rename it, and replace the title, date, and body.
  2. Add a new <li> to the top of the list in blog.html.

That's it. Upload the files and the post is live. The search box on the blog page picks up new entries automatically.
