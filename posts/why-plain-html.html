Why plain HTML?

Sep 14, 2026

A personal site is a handful of pages that change a few times a month. It doesn't need a bundler, a package manager, or three hundred megabytes of dependencies.

Plain HTML loads instantly, works without JavaScript, and will still open in a browser twenty years from now. When something breaks, there are only three files to look at.

Write the content. Style it once. Move on.
